
# Your Jobs USA

Your Jobs USA is a human-led federal job search and application support project. It helps job seekers organize their search, understand federal job announcements, connect their experience to role requirements, and prepare application materials for their own review.

The applicant makes the decisions. AI assists with drafting, organization, and interpretation.

## Project Principles

- Human-led decision making
- Applicant review before use or submission
- Clear explanation of federal job announcements
- Support for organizing qualifications and experience
- Responsible AI assistance
- Final application decisions remain with the applicant
